From brief to fully shipped.

A four-stage engagement that takes a brand from first conversation through launch and into ongoing optimization.

Step 01
Discovery
Week 1

Audit the current storefront, map brand goals to a build plan, surface the wins hiding inside what's already there. You leave this stage with a scoped roadmap, not a sales pitch.

  • Brand & positioning review
  • Tech audit + Core Web Vitals baseline
  • UX gap analysis
  • Build roadmap & timeline
Step 02
Build
Weeks 2–4

Custom Liquid theme work, performance tuning, content and catalog migration, app integration. Built in staged previews so you can see the storefront come together.

  • Custom Shopify theme in Liquid + JS
  • Catalog & content migration
  • App integration & customization
  • Performance & accessibility passes
Step 03
Launch & CRO
Weeks 5–8

Go-live, then immediately into measurement. A/B tests on highest-traffic templates, Klaviyo flows firing, technical SEO foundation laid down.

  • Production launch & QA
  • Klaviyo flows: welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase
  • A/B testing on PDP, cart, checkout
  • Technical SEO & schema baseline
Step 04
Ongoing Partnership
Months onward

Continuous optimization, support coverage, growth experiments, 3PL coordination.

  • Continuous CRO & design iteration
  • Email program & campaign management
  • SEO, B2B & affiliate growth
  • 3PL ops & CX support
